About North Star Bakery

North Star Bakery is meant to be a friendly neighborhood place. We want people to be able to get something fresh on the way to work or pick up bread for dinner without making a big trip out of it.

Ingredients

We try to use simple recipes, good flour and butter. We also try to buy local ingredients when they're available. Please tell us about any food allergies before placing an order.

People behind the counter

  • Our bread baker makes the loaves each morning.
  • The pastry baker handles cookies and breakfast pastries.
  • The front counter team helps with pickups and questions.
